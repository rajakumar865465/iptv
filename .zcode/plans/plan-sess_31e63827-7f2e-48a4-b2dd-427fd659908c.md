## Visual / design-system upgrades applied throughout

- **Depth:** layered radial indigo glows behind hero and section dividers; sections alternate `bg-white/[0.02]` with a subtle top/bottom hairline gradient instead of flat fills.
- **Typography:** enforce Poppins (`var(--font-poppins)`) on all headings via a shared `SectionHeading` component and a `.font-display` utility in `globals.css`. Tighten heading scale and use `text-balance` for wrap quality.
- **Cards:** consistent glass treatment — `bg-white/[0.04] border border-white/10 backdrop-blur-sm` with `hover:border-indigo-500/30 hover:bg-indigo-500/[0.04]` and a soft `hover:shadow-[0_0_40px_-12px] hover:shadow-indigo-500/30`.
- **Motion:** every major section fades/slides up on scroll via a reusable `MotionReveal` (framer-motion `whileInView`). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (disable transforms, keep opacity).
- **Live energy:** pulsing green "LIVE" dots reused on channel cards, ticker, and hero pill — consistent idiom.
- **Buttons:** primary = indigo gradient `from-indigo-600 to-blue-500` with hover lift; secondary = glass with white border. Consistent `min-h-[44px]` touch target.

## globals.css additions (additive only, no rewrites)

## Notes / risks
- Testimonials will be **placeholder content** until real reviews are provided — clearly marked with a comment for easy replacement.
- Comparison-table competitor details (cable/other-app pricing) will use reasonable generic figures with a small "approximate" footnote — no false claims.
- `force-dynamic` and server-side data fetching stay as-is; all new sections are server components except where motion requires `'use client'` (wrapped in `MotionReveal`, which is the only client boundary).
- Page remains fully responsive; comparison table collapses to stacked cards under `md`.

## Out of scope
